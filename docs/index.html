Chorus doesn't store what happened.
It stores what was believed — by whom, since when, on what grounds, and what became of that belief next.

Your agents already act on beliefs. They just can't show them to you. Ask a system today why it did something and you get a log of what it did — the belief that drove the act evaporated when the context window closed. Chorus is what you get when you refuse to let that evaporate.

Four load-bearing properties

Every belief is a signed claim — an author, a timestamp, a signature, forever. Disagreement is held in superposition instead of silently overwritten: two voices saying different things is a fact worth keeping, not a conflict to resolve behind your back. Trust is your editable policy, not the database's opinion — the same store reads differently under different judges, and both readings carry receipts. And every decision is replayable against exactly what was known when it was made: not what the store knows now — what it knew then, under the policy held then, verified by hash.

Memory is the feature. Belief accounting — the flight recorder for minds — is the product.

The instruments

An accounted mind can be examined. The store ships with a physician's kit:

chorus vitals
reads the chart — how contested, how stale, how much rests on one voice.
chorus diff
catches two stores drifting apart — and refuses to flatten agreement: the same conclusion from disjoint testimony is stronger than agreement, and it says so.
chorus bisect
binary-searches history to the instant a belief flipped — and who flipped it.
chorus examine
puts the measurements on the record, signed by an examiner you can distrust like anyone else.
chorus review
replays every standing decision; when the ground has moved, the decider gets a letter: "you acted on X; X is now known otherwise."
chorus challenge
asks stale beliefs to re-verify before they rot — in a grow-only store, re-assertion is re-verification.
chorus contradictions
finds one question answered in two vocabularies with two different answers — the contradiction no single-slot scan can see.
chorus skeptic
a resident doubter: files a signed doubt where everything rests on one voice, and withdraws it the moment a second voice corroborates.
chorus checkup
every dial in one pass. Exit 1 when anything wants a human.

None of them ever nags — an unchanged verdict is never re-mailed. And none of them mutates anything: hygiene here is testimony, not surgery. There is no view from nowhere, all the way up: the instruments live inside the epistemology they measure.

What it looks like

# one binary, many minds
chorus init
chorus store create mind
chorus remember svc:api owner team-a --store mind
chorus decide svc:api --intent "page team-a at 3am" --attribute owner --store mind

# the world moves…
chorus remember svc:api owner team-b --store mind

# …and the store speaks up
chorus review --store mind
reviewed 1 decision(s): 0 stand, 1 need revisiting.
  "page team-a at 3am" (svc:api)
    the question resolves differently today
    mail filed to its author

Serve any store over MCP and it becomes memory for whatever agent speaks the protocol — one session, one keypair, one voice in the chorus. Pin a snapshot and query it as GraphQL. Open the web console and read your own mind's mail.

Built on rhizomatic

Chorus is the product layer on @rhizomes/rhizomatic — a portable format of signed, content-addressed deltas whose merge is set union: order-blind, idempotent, grow-only. Nothing is ever edited in place; a retraction is itself a signed claim. The format is frozen by conformance vectors and two independent witnesses. The store can be trusted with a mind because the substrate cannot lie about what it was told.

Doubt that cannot be satisfied is not skepticism; it is a grudge. This skeptic can be satisfied.
